```
병아리와 함께 수학을 배웁니다. 유치원 수 세기부터 고등 수학까지 학년별로 이어지고, 묻기 전에 먼저 그림이나 도해로 보여 줍니다.

· 문장제는 나오는 사물이 그림으로 함께 나옵니다

■ 연습장

■ 14개 언어
설정에서 앱 언어를 바꿀 수 있습니다. 폰 언어를 바꾸지 않아도 됩니다.

■ 인터넷 없이
```

## 영어(미국) (en)

**앱 이름** (28자)
```
Peep Math - Counting to K-12
```
**간단한 설명** (72자)
```
From counting to high school math. Every question shows a picture first.
```
**자세한 설명**
```
Learn math with a chick. From counting in kindergarten to high-school topics, the grades follow one after another — and every question shows you a picture or diagram before it asks.

■ Kindergarten through grade 12
Each grade is split into units, and finishing one opens the next. Take the placement test if you would rather start where you actually are.

■ Questions you can see
· Counting — tap each item one by one as you count
· Shapes, clocks, fractions, number lines and bar charts are drawn on screen
· Word problems come with pictures of the things in them

■ Scratch pad
Work it out by hand right on top of the question. Change colour, erase, undo. It clears itself when you move on.

■ Skill by skill
Calculation, number sense, shapes, measurement, data and probability, and word problems are tracked separately so you can see what is weak.

■ Allowance wallet
Correct answers earn allowance. A parent can hand over real pocket money for it, protected by a PIN so a child cannot cash out alone. Spend what you keep on hearts, hints and stickers.

■ 14 languages
Change the app's language in Settings — no need to change the whole phone.

■ Works without internet
The only permission requested is vibration. Progress stays on the device and everything works in airplane mode.
```
